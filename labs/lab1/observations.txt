Lab 1 — Three observations from five tickets

1. The tickets contain noisy and messy text, including spelling mistakes,
   capital letters, forwarded email chains, signatures, phone numbers, and
   historical quoted content. This makes extracting the relevant information
   more difficult.

2. Some tickets may contain multiple policy-number-shaped strings, including
   numbers inside quoted replies. The system therefore needs to distinguish
   the current/live message from historical quoted content.

3. Category and urgency can be difficult to classify because similar-looking
   tickets can have different labels. In particular, an angry claim is still
   "claims" when the customer wants the claim processed, while "complaint"
   applies when Aurora's conduct itself is the subject. Urgency depends on the
   actual situation, escalation, and deadlines, not simply on how angry the
   customer sounds.
